    How to play
    • 13 rounds. On your turn, roll the five dice up to 3 times. Between rolls, choose dice to hold; held dice don't change.
    • Then score your dice in one empty box on your scorecard. Every box gets filled once, even if it scores 0.
    • Ones to Sixes: add up the dice showing that number. If those six boxes total 63 or more, you get a 35-point bonus.
    • Three of a Kind and Four of a Kind: add up all five dice. Full House (three of one number, two of another): 25. Small Straight (four in a row): 30. Large Straight (five in a row): 40. Chance: add up all five dice.
    • Five of a Kind: 50. If you roll another one after scoring 50 there, you get 100 bonus points, and it counts as a Full House or Straight if you want.
    • After all 13 rounds, the highest total wins.
    • Screen reader tip: each die is a button; choose it to hold or let go. The scorecard is a table: open boxes show a button with the points you'd score.
