        How to play
        • Goal: be first to have 3 full property sets of different colors on your table. Each property card says how many make a full set.
        • Your turn: you draw 2 cards (5 if your hand is empty), then make up to 3 plays, then End turn. You can hold at most 7 cards at the end of your turn.
        • A play is: put a property on your table; put a money or action card in your bank as money; or use an action card.
        • Rent: charge your opponent for a color you own. Double Rent, played with it, doubles it (each card is a play).
        • Swipe takes one property, Swap trades one for one (neither can be part of a full set). Set Steal takes a whole full set. Collect Debt is 5 million, Party Time is 2 million. Bonus Draw draws 2 cards. House adds 3 million rent to a full set, Hotel adds 4 more.
        • No Way! blocks an action played on you. Your opponent can play their own No Way! to block your block. It doesn't use a play.
        • Paying: you pay from your bank and your properties, never from your hand. No change is given. If you can't cover it, you pay everything you have. "Pay automatically" picks the smallest payment for you.
        • Wild properties stay the color you play them as. A full set needs at least one regular property.
        • Game options, chosen before dealing: Bank win (first to bank 30, 50 or 75 million wins) and Bankruptcy (if you owe money and can't pay it in full, you lose; it doesn't count during each player's first 3 turns).
        • Screen reader tip: choose a card in your hand to hear everything you can do with it. Each part of the table has its own heading.
        Deal Rush is an EvenPlay game. About EvenPlay.
