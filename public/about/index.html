Games for everyone

EvenPlay is a free site of card and dice games for two. No accounts and no downloads. Play a friend with a game code, find someone new, or practice against the computer, on a phone or a computer.

Every game is built so everyone gets the same seat at the table. You see real cards and dice. If you use a screen reader, every card, die and move is spoken. Blind and sighted players can sit down together and play on even terms. That's where the name comes from.

Who made it

EvenPlay was created by smoovgunner, a blind gamer who wanted games he could share with anyone, and built with Claude, an AI from Anthropic. Every game was tested by real players, with screen readers and without.
