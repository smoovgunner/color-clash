    How to play
    • One player creates a game and shares the code. The other types the code to join.
    • Match the top card by color or by number or symbol.
    • Wild cards can be played on anything. You pick the next color.
    • Skip and Reverse: with two players, you go again.
    • Draw Two and Wild Draw Four: your opponent draws, and you go again.
    • Can't play? Draw a card. If it fits you may play it, or pass.
    • First to empty their hand wins the round.
    • Screen reader tip: each card is a button. Cards you can't play right now say "can't play". Every move is announced, and each part of the table has its own heading.
    • Sound effects play for each move, and a chime plays when it's your turn. The Sound button at the top turns them off.
    Color Clash is an EvenPlay game. About EvenPlay.
