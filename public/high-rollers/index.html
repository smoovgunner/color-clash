        How to play
        • Six casinos, numbered 1 to 6. Each round, every casino gets cash bills until it holds at least $50,000. Each bill is a separate prize: the biggest is 1st place, the next is 2nd place, and so on. They are not added together.
        • Each player has 8 dice in their color, plus 4 white house dice. On your turn, roll all the dice you have left. Then pick one number you rolled and place every die showing that number, yours and white, on the casino with that number.
        • Take turns until all dice are placed. If your partner runs out first, you keep going.
        • Payout: at each casino, whoever has the most dice takes the biggest bill, the next most takes the next bill, and so on. The white house dice count as a third player; money they win leaves the game.
        • The twist: players who tie at a casino win nothing there, and the next player in line moves up. Use it to knock your rival out.
        • After 4 rounds, the most money wins. A tie goes to whoever has more bills.
        • Screen reader tip: after you roll, the Your roll section has one button per number, saying how many dice it places and what that casino holds. The Casinos section lists every casino's cash, dice and current leader.
