      How to play
      • We asked people a question. The board hides their most popular answers. Find them!
      • Face-off: the two of you take turns giving one answer. The higher answer on the board wins control. The number 1 answer wins control right away. If neither answer is on the board, face off again.
      • Control: keep answering. Each answer on the board adds its points to the round. A wrong answer is a strike.
      • Three strikes: the other player gets one answer to steal every point from the round.
      • Find every answer and the points are yours.
      • 4 rounds. Round 3 is worth double and round 4 is worth triple. Highest score wins.
      • Type your answer the way you'd say it. Close spellings, plurals and words like "a" or "the" are fine. Repeating an answer already on the board doesn't cost a strike.
      • No timer. Take your time.
