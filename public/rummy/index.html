      How to play
      • Each player gets 10 cards. On your turn: draw one card (from the pile, or the top of the discard pile), lay down or add to melds if you want, then discard one card.
      • A meld is a set (three or four cards of the same rank, like three 8s) or a run (three or more cards in a row in one suit, like 5, 6, 7 of Hearts). Aces are low: Ace, 2, 3 is a run; Queen, King, Ace is not.
      • You can add your cards to any meld on the table, yours or your opponent's.
      • You can't throw back the card you just took from the discard pile.
      • Go out by getting rid of every card, by melding or discarding. You score the points left in your opponent's hand: face cards 10, aces 1, other cards their number.
      • First to 100 points wins the match. If the draw pile runs out three times, the round is a draw.
      • How to play a move: select cards in your hand (tap a card to select it; tap again to unselect), then choose Lay down as a meld, Add to a meld, or Discard. The Hint button names the melds you could make; both players get it.
      Rummy is an EvenPlay game. About EvenPlay.
