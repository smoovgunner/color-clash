    How to play
    • There's one four-letter word in the middle. Change one letter to make a new real word, using a letter from one of your cards. GAME, then GATE, then LATE.
    • Every card has a letter on each side, and you can use either one. Blank cards can be any letter.
    • The quickest way: type the new word and choose Flip it. The game finds the right card for you. Or pick a card from your hand and choose where it goes.
    • One move per turn. Can't make a word? Draw a card. When the pile runs out, pass.
    • Careful: a word that isn't in the dictionary, or a word already played this round, costs you your turn.
    • Take turns game: the first player to use all their cards wins. If nobody can make a word, fewest cards wins.
    • Points game: every word scores its letters, as in classic word games (E or T is 1, Q or Z is 10). First to the target score wins. Run out of cards and you get 5 more.
    • Screen reader tip: the word, your cards and every move are announced. Each part of the table has its own heading.
    Letter Flip is an EvenPlay game. About EvenPlay.
